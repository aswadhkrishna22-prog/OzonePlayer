Ozone Player is an open-source Android music player built around music discovery, playlists, and local playback.

Features include:

• Browse music discovery and recommendation sections.
• Explore artist and playlist pages.
• Play music with a full-screen player and queue controls.
• View synchronized lyrics when available.
• Manage and play local audio files.
• Dual Play is an experimental beta feature and may change.

Ozone Player is based on ArchiveTune and is licensed under the GNU General Public License version 3.0.

Some online music sources and features may depend on third-party services, account access, network availability, and regional support.
